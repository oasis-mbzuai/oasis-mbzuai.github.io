Join

We are looking for PhD students at MBZUAI, across autonomous agents, software engineering, formal methods and security.

Who fits

People who enjoy both rigorous reasoning and hands-on systems building. You do not need every background on that list, and the best projects usually sit between two of them.

A practical filter: if you have ever been more interested in why a benchmark number was wrong than in improving it, you will like it here.

Getting in touch

Admission is decided by the university, not by this lab. PhD and MSc applications go through MBZUAI admissions, which is where the requirements, deadlines and funding are set out.

Writing to us is not an application, and it is not part of that decision. It is just a conversation, and usually a useful one to have before you start.

If you would like one, email {{ site.contact.email }} with your CV and a few lines on what you would like to work on.
